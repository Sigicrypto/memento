Step 3 — Action

Guest Camera Stream

Guests visit 📤 /upload/[slug]. Behind a frictionless gateway, they select photos, add names, and streams files directly to Supabase storage to populate the grid.

📂 App Location
  • app/upload/[slug]/page.tsx
